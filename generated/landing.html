Local-first moat discovery and eval scaffolding

Turn narrow workflow memory into something a team can inspect, test, and eventually defend.

automoat is a local-first project for turning messy business workflow data into structured artifacts: business-first discovery outputs, normalized records, and eval scaffolds that let a specific workflow claim become inspectable before heavier product or model work.

Broad Framing

What the product is trying to prove

The broader idea is not “AI for electricians.” It is that many businesses accumulate repeated judgment calls, local exceptions, and operational memory that never becomes inspectable. automoat is trying to turn that hidden workflow knowledge into artifacts a team can review, compare, and eventually productize.

  • Start from repeated business judgment, not model selection.
  • Use a narrow workflow wedge to prove the artifact contracts before widening the product surface.
  • Generate evidence-bearing discovery, normalization, and eval outputs before claiming product maturity.
Artifact Inventory

What exists on disk today

The generated folder shows both sides of the current MVP: business-first discovery outputs and dataset-first eval scaffolding. Every claim below maps to a file in this repo.

Discovery Runs Two generated Dallas business profiles exist: dallas-electrician-sample-v1 and dallas-electrician-south-dallas-v1. Each includes a business profile, workflow map, moat hypotheses, data-gap plan, eval opportunities, and a short operator summary.
Normalized Sample Three Dallas normalized datasets now exist: a synthetic scaffold under generated/normalized/dallas-electrician-sample-v1/ plus imported CSV normalization runs under generated/normalized/dallas-electrician-import-sample-v1/ and generated/normalized/dallas-electrician-import-sample-v2/. The wider v2 import currently carries 5 permits, 14 inspections, 24 source-record lineage rows, and explicit coverage for pass, fail, partial, cancelled, not_ready, and unknown inspection outcomes.
Eval Scaffold Three generated eval samples now exist. The synthetic scaffold has 14 tasks and 5 reviewed label rows. The imported v1 scaffold has 18 tasks and 7 reviewed label rows, while imported v2 expands to 20 tasks and 7 reviewed label rows across next_inspection_outcome, failure_reason_classification, recommended_next_action, and pattern_extraction.
Deterministic Writers The repo has thin local writers for discovery generation, fixture-pack generation, row-derived label reviews, eval generation, and raw CSV normalization. Current scripts are generate_dallas_discovery_artifacts.py, import_dallas_permit_extracts.py, generate_dallas_fixture_pack.py, generate_dallas_label_reviews.py, and generate_dallas_eval_artifacts.py.

Truth In Advertising

What is not built yet

Built

Narrow scope selection, artifact contracts, deterministic writers, two discovery variants, three normalized Dallas dataset paths, three eval scaffolds, and a shared unattended loop for keeping generated status surfaces current.

Not built

No large imported Dallas record set, no production application workflow, no live model benchmarking results, no cross-sample contract checker yet, and no evidence that the Dallas wedge produces a durable moat in practice.

Next best move: add a normalization or contract-check pass that compares synthetic and imported Dallas scaffolds, and carry source lineage plus optional rule documents through the same repeatable workflow.
