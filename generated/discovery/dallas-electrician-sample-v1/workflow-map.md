# Workflow Map

## Business

- Business: North Oak Residential Electric
- Scope: Dallas residential electrical permits and inspections
- Primary service area: East and northeast Dallas ZIP codes

## Current Workflow

### 1. Lead Intake

- Owner: office manager
- System: Jobber + inbound phone calls
- Repeated judgment: decide whether the job likely needs a permit before sending an estimator
- Delay point: permit-heavy jobs are sometimes quoted before the permit path is fully understood
- Data produced: customer info, address, rough scope
- Data saved well: partially

### 2. Estimating And Scope

- Owner: estimator or master electrician
- System: Jobber estimate, handwritten notes, phone photos
- Repeated judgment: determine whether the job is a service upgrade, remodel, repair, or miscellaneous electrical work
- Delay point: scope language is inconsistent, which makes later permit filtering hard
- Data produced: scope notes, estimated valuation, likely permit category
- Data saved well: partially

### 3. Permit Filing

- Owner: office manager
- System: Dallas permit portal plus Google Drive folder
- Repeated judgment: match the job to the right permit description and gather attachments
- Delay point: permit status is checked manually and follow-up timing is inconsistent
- Data produced: permit number, filing date, status snapshots
- Data saved well: yes, but spread across folders and emails

### 4. Work Execution

- Owner: field crew
- System: crew text threads, photos, job notes
- Repeated judgment: decide whether work is ready for inspection or still missing a correction item
- Delay point: field notes do not consistently roll back into a structured checklist
- Data produced: install photos, as-built observations, correction reminders
- Data saved well: weakly

### 5. Inspection Scheduling

- Owner: office manager
- System: permit portal, phone, email
- Repeated judgment: when to schedule and whether the site is truly ready
- Delay point: avoidable not-ready or fail outcomes when the office lacks complete field status
- Data produced: scheduled inspection date, type, result
- Data saved well: yes for dates, weakly for context

### 6. Failed Inspection Handling

- Owner: master electrician + office manager
- System: inspector notes, text threads, callback list
- Repeated judgment: interpret what the inspector really wants and translate it into crew actions
- Delay point: repeated failure reasons are not normalized, so the same lessons are relearned job by job
- Data produced: correction list, follow-up plan, reinspection timing
- Data saved well: poorly

### 7. Reinspection Or Closeout

- Owner: office manager + field lead
- System: permit portal, invoice packet, job notes
- Repeated judgment: verify whether the correction path is complete enough to pass final inspection
- Delay point: closeout checklists vary by crew and permit type
- Data produced: final inspection outcome, closeout status, callback avoidance notes
- Data saved well: final status yes, reusable lessons no

## Likely Moat-Producing Decisions

- Interpreting which failed inspection notes imply a service issue versus a documentation issue
- Knowing which jobs are likely to require a reinspection even if the crew believes the work is ready
- Turning neighborhood and job-type patterns into a pre-inspection checklist before the truck rolls
