# Data Gap Plan

## Priority Order

### 1. Failed Inspection Notes

- Source name: inspector result notes and office follow-up notes
- Why it matters: labels the failure reason and recommended-next-action tasks directly
- Availability: partly exists internally
- Collection difficulty: medium
- Privacy sensitivity: medium
- Expected lift for eval quality: high
- Recommended collection order: first

### 2. Reinspection Outcome History

- Source name: permit timeline with failed-to-passed sequences
- Why it matters: creates the clearest supervision for next action and approval-path analysis
- Availability: partly exists publicly and internally
- Collection difficulty: medium
- Privacy sensitivity: low
- Expected lift for eval quality: high
- Recommended collection order: second

### 3. Internal Correction Photos And Crew Notes

- Source name: photos, texts, and field notes attached to callbacks
- Why it matters: turns vague failures into concrete correction evidence
- Availability: exists internally but fragmented
- Collection difficulty: high
- Privacy sensitivity: medium
- Expected lift for eval quality: medium
- Recommended collection order: third

### 4. Dallas Rule And Guidance Snippets

- Source name: Dallas electrical inspection guidance, permit FAQs, and code reminder sheets
- Why it matters: supports retrieval and explanation quality for recommendations
- Availability: public
- Collection difficulty: low
- Privacy sensitivity: low
- Expected lift for eval quality: medium
- Recommended collection order: fourth

### 5. Permit Status Snapshots

- Source name: permit spreadsheet exports and portal status checks
- Why it matters: supports permit-state normalization and operational reporting
- Availability: exists internally and publicly
- Collection difficulty: low
- Privacy sensitivity: low
- Expected lift for eval quality: medium
- Recommended collection order: fifth

## Immediate Recommendation

Start with a reviewed sample of 25 to 50 failed or partial inspections where the business can reconstruct:

- permit type
- inspection type
- raw result note
- what correction was made
- whether the next inspection passed

That sample is enough to power the first recommended-next-action and failure-reason eval draft without waiting for full ingestion automation.
