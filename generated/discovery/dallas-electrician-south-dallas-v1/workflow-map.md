# Workflow Map

## Business

- Business: Red Brick Home Electric
- Scope: Dallas residential electrical permits and inspections
- Primary service area: South Dallas and Oak Cliff ZIP codes

## Current Workflow

### 1. Lead Intake

- Owner: dispatcher
- System: Housecall Pro + inbound calls
- Repeated judgment: decide whether an urgent service issue is likely to require a permit and inspection follow-up
- Delay point: older-home repair calls are sometimes booked before the permit path is understood
- Data produced: address, symptom description, urgency, probable work type
- Data saved well: partially

### 2. Scope Walkthrough

- Owner: estimator
- System: onsite notes, panel photos, Housecall Pro estimate
- Repeated judgment: decide whether the repair is simple replacement work or a larger service/rewire project
- Delay point: scope notes vary by estimator, which weakens later pattern analysis
- Data produced: scope summary, probable permit class, risk notes for older service equipment
- Data saved well: partially

### 3. Permit Preparation

- Owner: office manager
- System: Dallas permit portal + shared drive
- Repeated judgment: prepare the right permit description and supporting attachments for older-home work
- Delay point: permit folders capture forms but not the rationale behind permit choices
- Data produced: permit number, filing packet, status checks
- Data saved well: yes for files, weakly for decisions

### 4. Field Work

- Owner: field lead
- System: photo app, SMS, invoice notes
- Repeated judgment: decide whether discovered field conditions change what should be inspected
- Delay point: unexpected grounding or mast issues are noted in photos but not normalized
- Data produced: photos, material changes, correction prep notes
- Data saved well: weakly

### 5. Inspection Scheduling

- Owner: office manager
- System: portal, phone, email
- Repeated judgment: choose whether the site is ready or whether more prep is needed for older-home edge cases
- Delay point: the office lacks a repeatable checklist for common older-home correction patterns
- Data produced: inspection booking, date, result status
- Data saved well: yes for booking, weakly for readiness context

### 6. Correction Loop

- Owner: field lead + office manager
- System: inspector note, callback text, internal call log
- Repeated judgment: translate short failed inspection notes into the exact correction path for the crew
- Delay point: the same grounding and service corrections are relearned on repeat jobs
- Data produced: correction actions, reinspection target, crew follow-up note
- Data saved well: poorly

### 7. Reinspection And Closeout

- Owner: office manager
- System: portal timeline, invoice closeout note
- Repeated judgment: decide whether enough was corrected to avoid another trip
- Delay point: closeout notes summarize outcomes but do not preserve the winning correction path
- Data produced: pass/fail result, job closeout note, callback outcome
- Data saved well: final status yes, reusable lesson no

## Likely Moat-Producing Decisions

- Knowing which older-home service jobs need extra grounding and service-equipment checks before inspection scheduling
- Interpreting short failed inspection notes into a crew-ready correction sequence for older housing stock
- Recognizing ZIP-code-level housing patterns that change likely correction needs before the truck rolls
