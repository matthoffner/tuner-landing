Local-first moat discovery, evaluation, and build

Turn messy business data into a defensible AI moat.

`automoat` helps you discover what is actually proprietary in your business, turn that data into something testable, and prove whether it creates an advantage through research, benchmarks, retrieval, adaptation, and local-first workflows.

What It Does

Discover, define, prove, and operationalize a moat.

In the AI age, models get cheaper and more interchangeable. The durable advantage is the proprietary workflow data, decisions, edge cases, and operational context a business can turn into a system.

Business-First
Research the business, map moat ideas, plan what to test.
Dataset-First
Analyze records directly, generate evals, benchmark approaches.
Local-First
Useful on a real machine with private data and bounded workflows.
Artifact-Driven
Outputs plans, moat maps, evals, and recommendations, not just chat.
Current Focus

Dallas electricians MVP

The first proof slice is Dallas residential electrical permits and inspections: a narrow, real-world wedge for showing how local workflow records can become measurable operational advantage.

  • Chosen locality: Dallas, Texas
  • Chosen trade: electricians
  • Chosen workflow: residential permits and inspections
  • Current goal: the Dallas MVP now emits row-derived label_reviews.json from normalized permit and inspection rows; next is widening the normalized Dallas sample toward imported records without breaking downstream contracts
How It Works

1. Explore

Start with the business. Research the domain, identify likely moat candidates, and generate a plan for what data matters.

2. Analyze

Start with the dataset. Normalize records, identify what is unique, and turn it into a structured moat hypothesis.

3. Evaluate

Generate task-specific evals and compare baseline, retrieval-enhanced, and adapted approaches.

4. Build

Use the results to choose the right path: retrieval, adaptation, fine-tuning, or not a moat.

Use Cases

Not just one vertical.

The product can start from any business where boring records hide real judgment, process knowledge, or local operational patterns.

Insurance Claims manuals, exception memos, and policy interpretation patterns.
Legal Memos, rulings, argument style, and internal risk interpretation.
Compliance Regulated workflows, quality records, and high-trust internal process memory.
Trades Permits, inspections, failures, and local code interpretation.
Property Ops Violations, repairs, approvals, and neighborhood-specific patterns.
Founder Discovery Weird niche datasets that may hide a product, API, or workflow moat.
